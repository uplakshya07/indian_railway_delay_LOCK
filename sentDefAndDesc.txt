I am writing to submit the definition and description of my Data Warehousing and Mining project titled "Indian Railways Punctuality & Network Delay Dynamics Analysis"

Definition: Indian Railways generates massive volumes of operational data daily -- scheduled timetables, station master records, and actual arrival/departure logs. Train delays across extensive rail networks disrupt travel schedules, reduce operational efficiency, and cascade into system-wide congestion. This project aims to analyze historical train delay data to uncover underlying punctuality patterns, pinpoint high-risk stations and bottlenecks, and forecast delays to enable proactive operational and commuter planning.

Description: This project focuses on evaluating a comprehensive dataset of passenger train operations across the Indian Railways network over a full year. By analyzing scheduled timetables, station sequences, and recorded delays across multiple service types, the project addresses three primary objectives:
- Punctuality & Temporal Pattern Analysis: Characterizing delay distributions across different times of the year, days of the week, and train categories (e.g., Express, Superfast, Premium services).
- Bottleneck & High-Risk Route Identification: Mapping geographical and structural stress points across railway zones, identifying critical transit stations and sections that consistently trigger or amplify delays.
- Delay Prediction: Forecasting anticipated arrival and departure delays at subsequent stations along a route, transforming raw operational records into actionable foresight.
