DATASET
I am using the Indian Railways Delay Dataset from Kaggle (https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/rxydenxd/indian-railways-delay-dataset). It covers one year of operations (8 Feb 2025 to 7 Feb 2026) in four linked tables: train details (about 9,000 trains across 8 service categories), station master (about 9,000 stations with railway zone), the full timetable schedule (172,112 stops), and daily station-level delay records in minutes (38.4 million rows).

WORK DONE SO FAR (Data Understanding and Preprocessing)
1. Profiled all four tables: record counts, missing and unique values, value distributions and attribute types.
2. Data quality checks found the following:
   - About 4.9% of delay values are missing. The gaps are structured, not random: some stations have no delay data for the whole year, and a few days in February 2025 have 12 to 13% missing delays.
   - train_no is not unique in the train master (8,992 rows but only 8,720 unique numbers).
   - 64 stations in the schedule and delay data do not exist in the station master.
3. Key resolution: I resolved the duplicate train numbers, which came from premium-service overlay tags, into one clean train dimension of 8,720 trains. The unmatched stations were added to the station dimension so no records were lost. I also verified, across all 38.4 million rows using DuckDB, that (date, train, stop sequence) uniquely identifies a delay record, and defined it as the grain of the delay fact table.

The notebooks and cleaned dimension tables are ready.

Phase 1: Final ETL & Data Cleaning: This covers rules for missing station addresses and schedule times, handling of structurally missing delays, and smoothing of outlier delay values.
