Because we cannot use forward-filling for origin stations (there is no previous station to pull data from), we have to handle missing origin delays differently than mid-route gaps. Here is how we should adjust the imputation strategy based on your correction:

For Origin Stations (station_no = 1): Use a class-specific central tendency. Replace the missing departure delay with the median departure delay calculated exclusively for that specific train_no, or the median departure delay for that specific station_name.

For Intermediate/Destination Stations (station_no > 1): Use spatiotemporal interpolation (forward-filling) to carry the known delay forward from the train's preceding station.

For Completely Missing Sequences: Apply the Cancellation Drop Rule. If the delay is missing at the origin and remains missing for the entire journey, drop the tuples entirely, as the train was likely cancelled.
